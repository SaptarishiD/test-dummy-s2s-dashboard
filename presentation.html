04 · EFI

The full distribution, weighted toward both tails

EFI = (2/π) ∫01 [p − Ff(qc(p))] / √[p(1−p)] dp

Forecast-member fractions are evaluated at 101 model-climate percentiles. The tail weight makes departures near 0 and 1 matter more.

−1−0.8−0.50+0.5+0.8+1

Positive: unusually wet or warm. Negative: unusually dry or cool.

Interpretation limitEFI is not probability, calibration, skill, warning severity, or guaranteed impact.

05 · Advanced diagnostic

SOT asks whether the forecast tail moves beyond the climate tail

Upper tail

(forecast q90 − climate q99) / (climate q99 − climate q90)

Used for heavy rainfall and warm temperature.

Lower tail

(forecast q10 − climate q01) / (climate q01 − climate q10)

Used only for cold temperature.

<−1−1–00–0.50.5–11–2>2

Values are not clipped. A zero or degenerate climate-tail width is published as unavailable. SOT is intentionally placed under “Advanced maps”.
